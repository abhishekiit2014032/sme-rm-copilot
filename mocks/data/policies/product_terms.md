## §2.1 Working capital limit tenor
A working capital limit has a 12 month tenor and is renewed at the annual review.

## §3.1 Import invoice financing
Import invoice financing pays suppliers against import invoices for up to 120 days, for up to 90% of the invoice value.

## §3.2 Business fixed deposit
A business fixed deposit needs a minimum amount of AED 100,000 and a term of 1 to 12 months.
