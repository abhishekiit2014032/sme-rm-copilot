## §4.1 Scope
This section applies to working capital facilities for SME borrowers with annual turnover up to AED 250m.

## §4.2 Unsecured lending cap
The total unsecured working capital limit for one SME borrower must not exceed AED 2.5m. An unsecured limit also needs a risk rating of 5 or better.

## §4.3 Financial statements
Audited financial statements not older than 18 months are required for any working capital limit above AED 1m.

## §4.4 Utilisation review
The RM reviews any working capital limit that stays above 90% utilised for three consecutive months.

## §4.5 Limits above the unsecured cap
A working capital limit above the unsecured cap in §4.2 needs acceptable collateral under §4.6 for the amount above the cap, or an exception approved by the Credit Committee.

## §4.6 Acceptable collateral
Acceptable collateral for SME working capital limits is:
- property mortgage (first degree, UAE property, counted at 70% of valuation)
- cash margin (held with the Bank, counted at 100%)
- assignment of receivables (approved debtors only, counted at 60%)
Personal guarantees support a limit but do not count as collateral.

## §9.1 Pricing floors {clearance: restricted}
Minimum margins by risk grade are set by the Credit Risk function and are not shared with RMs.
