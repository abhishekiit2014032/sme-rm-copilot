## §1.1 Limit increase documents
A limit increase request needs audited financial statements for the last two years, six months of bank statements, a valid trade licence and the signed limit application form.

## §1.2 New trade line documents
A new trade line needs the trade licence, the customer's import and export history for twelve months and the signed trade finance agreement.
