OUR SLO

Pilot 99.0% in business hours; flagged degraded and partial answers count as available
p95: UC1 ≤ 8 s · UC2 ≤ 7 s · UC3 draft ≤ 10 s · commit ≤ 2 s

Need = availability Mon to Fri, 07:00 to 20:00 GST, to agree with each system owner. Timeouts never exceed the deadline left.

Every dependency gets an interface agreement, and this slide is the summary of that document. Read it row by row: what we call, the availability we need in business hours, the p95 latency we budget, how we time out and retry, and what the RM sees if the system is down. Only one Bank system is critical: the CRM. It holds the customer profile and the portfolio used for entitlement checks, so if it is down the brief and the follow-up stop, and approved writes wait in the outbox and post when it is back. That is why we need 99.8% from the CRM in the pilot. Everything else degrades. If the core banking ODS, lending, KYC or the news feed fails, the brief is still built and that section is flagged as unavailable, which counts as available because it is honest and useful. If Graph is down, the RM opens the brief from the CRM record instead. Timeouts are derived from the request deadline that web-bff sets: each call gets the smaller of its own cap and the time left. Reads get at most one retry and only if it fits; writes never retry blindly, they go through the outbox with a read before retry. For Azure OpenAI the published SLA is 99.9%, but model loss is not an outage for us: the copilot drops to degraded mode, a data-only brief, ranked policy passages without a generated answer, or a manual call report. A second deployment in UAE North is tried only if at least 4 seconds remain. Our own promise: 99.0% in business hours for the pilot, with p95 latency of 8 seconds for the brief, 7 for a policy answer, 10 for follow-up drafts and 2 for a commit, not counting any step-up sign-in. The 99.5% target for scale is committed only after three months of pilot measurements, and it needs the CRM at 99.9% and Event Hubs Premium. Figures are targets to agree with each system owner in discovery; the composite maths is in the NFR document.
