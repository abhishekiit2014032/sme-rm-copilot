3 · DELIVERY APPROACH

Start small, prove value, then scale

Weeks 0–4

Discover and set up

  • Time-and-motion baseline
  • API and data inventory
  • Residency, outsourcing and abuse-monitoring approvals
  • Golden test set with SMEs

Gate: approved business case and data access

Weeks 5–16

Pilot, 30 RMs

  • UC2 policy copilot first
  • Then UC1 brief and UC3 with RM approval
  • CRM sidebar and Teams tab
  • Weekly evals and feedback

Gate: 60%+ weekly use, 95%+ grounded, no P1 incidents

Weeks 17–28

Scale to 300 RMs

  • All SME RMs, Arabic GA
  • Voice memos for UC3
  • PTU if justified; Teams bot after residency sign-off
  • Hand-over to run team

Gate: KPI targets met, audit review passed

Month 8+

Extend

  • Proactive alerts and next-best actions
  • Credit memo drafting
  • Other segments

Gate: new use-case approval by the AI board

The roadmap is gated, not date-driven. Discovery takes four weeks and produces the measured baseline, the API inventory and the approvals that usually cause delays: data residency, outsourcing and the Microsoft abuse-monitoring exemption, so they are on the critical path from day one. The pilot with 30 RMs starts with the policy copilot because it needs only documents, not customer data integrations, so we can show value within weeks while the APIs for the brief are being connected. The brief and the follow-up agent follow within the pilot, and follow-up writes to the CRM only ever happen after the RM approves, with a signed approval. Scale to 300 RMs brings Arabic, voice memos, provisioned throughput if the measured volume justifies it, and a Teams bot only after a residency sign-off. Each phase ends with explicit go/no-go criteria agreed with the business sponsor, risk and IT. Team for the pilot: product owner from SME banking, an AI engineer pair, a data/integration engineer, a QA and evaluation analyst, and part-time risk and InfoSec partners.
