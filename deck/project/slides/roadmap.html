3 · DELIVERY APPROACH

Start small, prove value, then scale

Weeks 0–6

Discover and set up

  • Time-and-motion baseline
  • API and data inventory
  • Security and model-risk approvals
  • Golden test sets with SMEs

Gate: approved business case and data access

Weeks 7–18

Pilot, read-only

  • UC2 policy copilot first
  • UC1 brief from week 11
  • ~25 RMs, 2 regions
  • Weekly evals and feedback

Gate: 60%+ weekly use, 95%+ grounded, no P1 incidents

Weeks 19–30

Scale and write back

  • UC3 with approval flow
  • All SME RMs
  • Arabic, Teams and mobile
  • Hand-over to run team

Gate: KPI targets met, audit review passed

Month 8+

Extend

  • Proactive alerts and next-best actions
  • Credit memo drafting
  • Other segments

Gate: new use-case approval by the AI board

The roadmap is gated, not date-driven. Discovery produces the measured baseline, the API inventory and the approvals that usually cause delays, so they are on the critical path from day one. The pilot starts with the policy copilot because it needs only documents, not customer data integrations, so we can show value within weeks while the APIs for the brief are being connected. Everything in the pilot is read-only; write-back to the CRM comes in phase 2, once trust and controls are proven. Each phase ends with explicit go/no-go criteria agreed with the business sponsor, risk and IT. Team for the pilot: product owner from SME banking, an AI engineer pair, a data/integration engineer, a QA and evaluation analyst, and part-time risk and InfoSec partners.
