USE CASE 2 · ANSWER

Policy answers with the clause attached

How it works

1. RM asks in plain language, in English or Arabic

2. Hybrid search (keyword + vector) over approved policies, product guides and circulars, filtered to current versions the RM may see

3. Top passages are reranked; the LLM answers only from them and cites every claim

4. Groundedness check; weak evidence means “I can’t confirm this” and a hand-off to the credit desk

“Can a 2-year-old trading company get an unsecured working-capital line of AED 750k?”

Not under the standard programme. Unsecured SME working capital needs at least 3 years of audited trading history [1]. Options: a receivables-backed line, available from 2 years of trading [2], or a policy exception approved at Credit Risk level 2 [3].

[1] SME Credit Policy v4.2 §5.3.1, effective 01 Jul 2026

[2] Receivables Finance product guide, p.2

[3] Delegation of Authority matrix, row 14

DATA USED

Credit policy, product guides, pricing grids, authority matrix, circulars

TARGET

30–40% fewer escalations

This is classic retrieval-augmented generation, but with three bank-grade additions. First, only approved, versioned documents are indexed, and retrieval filters on effective date and document classification, so the RM never gets an answer from a superseded policy. Second, every sentence must carry a citation to a specific clause, and a groundedness check verifies that the cited text actually supports the claim. Third, when evidence is weak the assistant says so and routes the question to a human instead of guessing. The document names and clause numbers here are illustrative.
