Metrics are layered so we can tell why something is or is not working. Adoption comes first: if RMs do not use it, nothing else matters. Productivity measures the time we promised to give back. Quality and trust are the guardrail metrics; a drop there pauses rollout regardless of adoption. Business outcomes are the reason to do this, but they lag, so we measure them against a control group of comparable RMs without the copilot during the pilot to separate the copilot’s effect from seasonality and market conditions. All targets are proposals to be agreed with the sponsor after the discovery baseline.
