WHY HYBRID

Policy questions mix exact terms (product codes, clause numbers) with meaning. Keyword plus vector search beats either one alone.

Most RAG failures in banks come from the ingestion side, not the model. Policies have tables, cross-references, numbered clauses and superseded versions, so we chunk by clause and keep the heading path, and we tag every chunk with its version, effective date and classification. On the query side, the filter step is the important one: retrieval only considers current versions the user is cleared to see, so the model cannot cite a withdrawn policy. Reranking gives a large precision gain for little cost. The final verify step checks each sentence of the answer against the cited passages; if support is weak, the assistant declines and offers to route the question to the credit or product desk.
