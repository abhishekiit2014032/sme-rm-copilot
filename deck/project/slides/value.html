The value case is built bottom-up from activity, not from a vendor benchmark. It is deliberately more conservative than the use-case targets: UC1 aims to save 35 minutes of prep per meeting, but the value model assumes only about 20 minutes per meeting across prep and write-up, roughly half the target, to allow for uneven adoption. An SME RM with around 8 customer meetings a week saving about 20 minutes per meeting on preparation and write-up, plus about an hour and a half of policy look-ups, gets roughly 4 hours a week back. Across 50 RMs that is around 210 hours, about 5 full-time equivalents of selling capacity, without hiring. The harder-to-quantify benefits are just as important: better-prepared conversations, same-day call reports that improve pipeline and risk visibility, and consistent application of credit policy. Every input here will be replaced with measured baselines in discovery.
