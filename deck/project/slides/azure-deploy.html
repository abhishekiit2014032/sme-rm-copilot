Where it runs. One Azure region, UAE North, across three availability zones, and nothing has a public endpoint. On the left is the Bank: systems of record in its data centre, and RMs on managed devices, either on the corporate network or through Entra Private Access. ExpressRoute private peering connects it to the hub. The hub is the Bank's shared landing zone: the ExpressRoute gateway, Azure Firewall with an egress allow-list, Bastion with PIM for break-glass admin, and private DNS zones. The copilot has its own spoke. The internal Application Gateway with WAF is the only way in. The AKS private cluster spreads its node pools over the three zones. APIM Premium runs in internal VNet mode with two units across zones. Every PaaS service is reached through a private endpoint: Azure OpenAI, AI Search, Cosmos DB, Key Vault, Event Hubs, Storage and App Configuration. Telemetry leaves through the Azure Monitor private link scope. The only other egress is Entra ID and Graph on the Microsoft backbone and the licensed news feed through the firewall. Resilience is zone redundancy. Losing the whole region is treated as a business continuity event, not hot disaster recovery: UAE Central is a restricted region, so we keep a geo-redundant copy of the audit store and documents, geo backups of Cosmos DB, and rebuild the app tier from infrastructure as code, with a best-effort recovery time of 24 hours while RMs fall back to their normal CRM screens. There are four environments, dev, test, pre-prod and prod. Only prod sees real data, and each environment has its own Azure OpenAI resource.
