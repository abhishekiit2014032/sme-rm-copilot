Four decisions to start discovery

1 · SPONSOR AND COHORT

Name the business sponsor and the ~25 pilot RMs across two regions

2 · DATA ACCESS

API catalogue, sample policy documents and a sandbox environment

3 · RISK PARTNERS

Model risk, compliance and InfoSec contacts engaged from week one

4 · SUCCESS CRITERIA

Agree the metrics, baselines and go/no-go gates for the pilot

Questions and discussion

Close by turning the proposal into decisions. The four asks are what unblocks discovery: a sponsor and a pilot cohort, access to APIs, documents and a sandbox, named partners from risk and compliance, and agreement on how success will be judged. Then open the floor for questions. Useful questions to prompt if the room is quiet: which core systems already have APIs, how the bank’s model risk policy treats LLMs today, and which RM teams would volunteer for the pilot.
