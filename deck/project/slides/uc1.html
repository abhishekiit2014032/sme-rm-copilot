USE CASE 1 · PREPARE

A customer brief in under a minute

How it works

1. RM asks: “Prep me for Harbourline Logistics, 10:00 tomorrow”

2. Agent checks the customer is in the RM’s portfolio, then calls read-only tools in parallel

3. Figures come straight from tool output; the LLM only summarises and ranks what matters

4. Brief appears in the CRM sidebar or Teams, with a source link on every line

Harbourline Logistics LLC (fictional) · 10:00 meeting

Snapshot: customer since 2019 · turnover AED 48m · 3 facilities · revenue to bank +12% YoY

Watch: working-capital line 92% utilised for 45 days · trade licence renewal due 14 Dec

Opportunity: outbound FX payments +38% this quarter, so discuss FX forwards

Talking points: limit headroom for Q1 · renewed licence · FX desk intro

Sources: CRM #4471 · Core banking · LOS facility F-209 · KYC file · FX payments ledger

DATA USED

CRM, core banking, loan origination, trade finance, KYC/CDD, external news

TARGET

Prep ~45 → ~10 min per meeting

Walk the flow left to right. The important design point is step 2: the agent does not search a big index of customer data. It calls the same APIs the CRM uses, with the RM’s own identity, so it can only see customers that RM is entitled to. Step 3 is the main hallucination control for this use case: every number in the brief is copied from a tool response, not generated, and the LLM’s job is to summarise, prioritise and suggest talking points. The customer shown is fictional. The 45-to-10-minute figure is a target to baseline in discovery.
