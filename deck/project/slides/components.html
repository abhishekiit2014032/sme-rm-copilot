2 · AI / LLM COMPONENTS

The right model for each job, all behind one gateway

Generation

Frontier LLM

Planning, summarising and drafting. Private, in-region endpoint; no training on bank data.

Routing

Small, fast LLM

Intent routing, classification and field extraction, at a fraction of the cost and latency.

Retrieval

Embeddings + reranker

Multilingual (English and Arabic) embeddings and a cross-encoder reranker for precision.

Voice

Speech-to-text

Transcribes RM voice memos for UC3 from Phase 2. In-region; audio deleted after use.

Safety

Guardrail models

PII detection and masking, prompt-injection and off-topic classifiers on input and output.

Quality

Evaluator (LLM judge)

Scores groundedness and answer quality, calibrated against labels from credit SMEs.

The architecture is deliberately model-agnostic: every model sits behind the LLM gateway, so we can swap or add models without touching the use cases. In practice we use a tiered approach: a frontier-class model where reasoning and writing quality matter (the brief, policy answers, drafts), and a smaller, cheaper model for high-volume, simple tasks like routing and extraction. Retrieval quality is driven as much by the embedding model and reranker as by the LLM, and Arabic support has to be tested explicitly. The guardrail and evaluator models are what let us measure and enforce quality, not just hope for it. On Azure these are Azure OpenAI deployments in Azure AI Foundry (gen-large, gen-small and embed) behind the APIM AI gateway, plus Content Safety, AI Language and AI Search; exact model versions are confirmed with Microsoft for UAE North.
