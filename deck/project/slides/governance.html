4 · MONITORING AND GOVERNANCE

Governed like any other model in the bank

FIRST LINE · PRODUCT TEAM

Owns quality: evals, monitoring, model cards, prompt and index versioning, incident response.

SECOND LINE · RISK

Model risk, compliance and InfoSec validate each use case before go-live and review it periodically.

THIRD LINE · AUDIT

Internal audit can replay any answer for 1 year; the immutable metadata trail is kept 7 years.

Live monitoring

  • Latency, cost and token use per use case
  • Groundedness and decline rates (sampled)
  • Tool errors and entitlement denials
  • Retrieval drift as policies change

Change and incident control

  • Every prompt, model and index change runs the eval suite
  • AI governance board approves new use cases
  • Kill switch and rollback per use case
  • Aligned to the regulator’s AI and data-protection rules

The copilot is treated as a model under the bank’s existing model risk framework, not as a side project. The three lines of defence map naturally: the product team owns quality day to day; model risk, compliance and InfoSec validate each use case before it goes live and review it periodically; internal audit can reconstruct any answer for a year from the encrypted audit content store, and the tamper-evident metadata trail, with sources and the exact model and prompt version, is kept for seven years. Monitoring covers both operations (latency, cost, errors) and quality (groundedness, declines, drift). Every change, including a prompt tweak or a policy re-index, goes through the same evaluation gate, and every use case has its own kill switch. Specific regulatory references, such as the central bank’s guidance and data-protection law, are confirmed with the bank’s compliance team.
