1 · USE CASES

Three use cases covering the RM’s meeting cycle

UC1

Agentic retrieval

Pre-meeting customer brief

A one-page brief in under a minute: relationship snapshot, recent activity, limits, KYC and covenant flags, and suggested talking points.

EXPECTED BENEFIT

Prep time per meeting from ~45 to ~10 minutes (target)

UC2

RAG + citations

Policy and product copilot

Plain-language answers on credit policy, eligibility, pricing and procedures. Each answer cites the clause and version it came from.

EXPECTED BENEFIT

30–40% fewer RM escalations to credit and product teams (target)

UC3

Agent + human approval

Post-meeting follow-up agent

Turns notes or a voice memo into a call report, tasks and a draft email, and suggests next-best actions. The RM approves before anything is saved.

EXPECTED BENEFIT

Over 90% of call reports logged the same day (target)

Three use cases that together cover the whole meeting cycle: before (prepare), during or between meetings (answer) and after (follow up). They were chosen because each one is high-frequency, low-regret and buildable on data the bank already has. Each one also uses a different AI pattern, which lets the pilot prove the platform end to end: live tool calls, RAG over documents, and an agent that proposes writes for a human to approve. Note what is deliberately out of scope for phase 1: the assistant never makes credit decisions, never prices a deal and never sends anything to a customer without the RM approving it.
