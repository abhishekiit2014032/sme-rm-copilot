EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

One assistant, three jobs, a pilot in under six months

THE PROBLEM

SME RMs lose a large share of their week to finding information, preparing for meetings and post-meeting admin, time that should go to customers. We estimate 40–50% (to validate in discovery).

THE SOLUTION

An AI copilot inside the CRM and Teams that prepares a customer brief, answers policy and product questions with sources, and follows up by drafting call reports, tasks and emails.

HOW IT IS BUILT

An orchestrator agent calls entitlement-checked APIs for live customer data and RAG for policy documents. A governed LLM gateway sits in front of the model, and the RM approves every write.

THE PLAN AND THE PRIZE

6-week discovery, then a 12-week pilot with about 25 RMs, then scale. Target: 4+ hours back per RM per week, same-day call reports and faster credit turnaround.

If the audience remembers one slide, it is this one. The problem: RM time is being spent on information retrieval and admin rather than customers. The solution is one assistant with three clearly scoped use cases, each with a different AI pattern: agentic retrieval for the brief, RAG for policy Q&A, and an agent with human approval for follow-ups. Architecturally, the key point is that customer data is never dumped into a vector store: it is fetched live through APIs that enforce the RM’s own entitlements. The delivery plan is deliberately small at first, with one pilot cohort, measurable targets and clear go/no-go gates.
