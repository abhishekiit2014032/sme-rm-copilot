RM Copilot

Prototype · fictional data

Ask for a brief on any customer. The copilot checks you cover that customer, calls read-only tools, and builds a one-page brief where every figure comes from a tool, not the model.

Ask a credit-policy or product question. Answers come only from approved, current documents, with a citation on every claim. Weak evidence means a decline, not a guess.

Paste or type meeting notes. The copilot drafts a call report, tasks, next-best actions and a customer email. Nothing is saved until you approve.

  1. Run a request to see guardrails, entitlement checks, tool calls, retrieval and verification.
Every step is written to the audit log.
This prototype runs entirely in the browser with fictional customers and fictional policy excerpts. The model steps are simulated with deterministic logic so the demo always behaves the same way; in production, those steps call an LLM through the bank’s LLM gateway. The controls around them (entitlements, version filters, citations, checks and approval) work as they would in production.
